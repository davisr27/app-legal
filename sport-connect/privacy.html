Zelkivra — Privacy Policy

Network beta · Last updated: 14 October 2026

Zelkivra is published by Yuta Kodama. Davis Robinson is the support contact. The network app uses a shared service so players can organise matches, join rosters, exchange messages and run competitions across devices. This policy covers that network build and the launch website.

Accounts and shared information

The service stores your account identifier, username, display name, selected sports, skill and position preferences, and organiser settings. Passwords and account-recovery codes are stored as hashes, not readable passwords or codes. Sign-in uses short-lived access tokens and rotating refresh credentials. The service stores credential hashes, expiry, session-family identifiers and refresh request identifiers, not readable tokens. The app keeps its credentials in device-only iOS Keychain storage. Access tokens last 15 minutes; refresh credentials can renew access for up to 30 days. Signing out revokes that device's session family. Password recovery, suspension and account deletion revoke all account sessions.

When Sign in with Apple is configured, Apple verifies your sign-in and sends signed identity information to the shared service. The service discards email/name claims, identity tokens and one-use authorization codes after verification. It retains a hash of the app-specific Apple account identifier and an encrypted Apple refresh grant to validate authorization and revoke it when you delete your account. The device stores Apple's private account identifier in device-only Keychain to check authorization changes. This identifier is not exposed in your public profile. You enter the public name you want other players to see.

New Apple setup expires after ten minutes unless completed. Cancelled or expired setup grants are queued for revocation when no active setup or linked account still needs them. After account deletion, the encrypted grant and identifier hash may remain in a minimal retry queue until Apple confirms revocation; local account/content deletion proceeds even when Apple is temporarily unavailable. Operator backups may retain earlier copies for up to 30 days. App session credentials remain hash-only on the service; encrypted Apple grants are distinct from those credentials.

The service also stores matches and venues you create or join, membership and waiting-list records, team assignments, fixtures, results, attendance and payment-status marks, group messages, blocks and support reports. These records support the app's features and account recovery on another device. The app does not process payments or collect card or bank details.

Public match details are discoverable by signed-in players. Private events require an invitation. Group messages are available to authorised event members. Other members can see the profile and roster information needed to organise the event; your password, recovery code and sign-in token are never shared with them.

The service saves your latest read position in each match chat so unread counts follow you across devices. It stores the message reference, read order and update time for app functionality. A message sender can see an aggregate count of current, active, unblocked members who have read that far, without individual reader names or read times. Leaving or being removed from a match deletes your read position; deleting your account removes all of your read positions.

Location, Maps and Calendar

Your device location is used on the device to centre maps and calculate distances. When nearby discovery is used, the current search coordinates and selected radius may also be sent to the shared service to find nearby events. The supplied backend processes these coordinates for the request and does not save a device-location history. The venue address and coordinates you choose for an event are stored with that event; joining or checking in links your account to that venue. Apple Maps processes its map and venue-search requests under Apple's privacy policy.

Nearby game alerts are optional. If you choose “Notify me within 10 km,” the service saves one location you selected, linked to your account, to find newly posted public games within 10 km. This area does not follow you as you move; you must explicitly replace it. Only you can retrieve your saved alert area. Turn alerts off in Profile → Settings → Nearby game alerts to delete the saved area and queued discovery alerts, or delete your account. Push delivery requires notification permission and a registered device. Alerts contain a generic notice and event reference, without your saved coordinates, venue address or other players' names/messages. Blocked organisers and private, cancelled or started matches are excluded.

Calendar access is optional. The app checks clashes on the device and can add a match to your Calendar. Your calendar contents are not uploaded to the Zelkivra service. Calendar events may sync through the accounts you have configured in iOS.

Notifications

Match reminders can be scheduled locally. When remote notifications are configured and you allow them, the service stores an Apple push token to deliver event updates through Apple's notification service. You can turn notifications off in iOS Settings. Shared data also refreshes while the app is open.

Reporting and blocking

Blocks are stored with your account so they apply across devices. Reports send the selected account or content, reason and any explanation to the operator's moderation queue. The operator can review reports, remove content and suspend accounts. Do not include unnecessary private information in report text.

Tournament Hosting subscriptions

Tournament Hosting is an optional monthly Apple subscription for unlimited tournament creation. Joining competitions, hosting ordinary matches and creating leagues remain free. Apple handles purchases, renewal, payment details and cancellation under its own terms. The app associates the purchase with the Zelkivra account used to subscribe by sending that account's identifier to Apple as an app account token.

The app sends Apple's signed transaction to the authenticated shared service. The service verifies it and checks current subscription status with Apple; it stores the original transaction reference linked to your account so access can be refreshed. It does not store card details or the raw signed transaction. This purchase information is used only to provide subscription functionality, not advertising or tracking. You can restore purchases or manage/cancel the subscription in Profile → Settings → Tournament Hosting. Use the same Zelkivra account to restore.

Deleting your Zelkivra account removes its stored purchase reference but does not cancel Apple's subscription. Cancel in Apple's subscription management before deleting your account. Cancelling stops new tournament creation when access expires; existing tournaments remain available to finish.

Storage, deletion and sharing

The device caches authorised records for display. The shared service stores active account and event records on its hosting infrastructure. A network connection is required to confirm shared changes; a cached screen does not confirm a new join or message.

Account deletion in Profile → Settings removes your account credentials and sessions from the active service, removes your memberships, and removes or anonymises content associated with your account. Shared events may pass to another member so the group can continue. Shared venue details, schedules, other members' messages and aggregate results remain available to the remaining participants; deleted account identifiers are removed from results. Existing device or server backups, if maintained, may retain earlier copies until those backups are replaced or deleted.

Active account and event records are retained while needed to provide the service, until you delete your account or an organiser removes an event. Match chat is limited to its latest 2,000 messages. Backup copies, when created, are retained for up to 30 days. A restored backup must replay account-deletion requests before the service is made available.

Signing out clears the account session on this device; it does not delete the shared account. Deleting the app alone does not delete the account. Information you deliberately share through the iOS share sheet is handled by the app or person you choose.

The website and support

The optional website waitlist stores the email address you submit so the operator can send the launch updates you requested. To request removal, contact the address below. Support email is handled by the email providers involved. Please send only the information needed to resolve your request.

Hosting providers process the network information needed to serve requests, such as IP addresses. The app includes no advertising, analytics or third-party tracking SDK and does not sell personal information.
