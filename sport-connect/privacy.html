Sports Connect — Privacy Policy

Network beta · Last updated: 8 October 2026

Sports Connect is published by Yuta Kodama. Davis Robinson is the support contact. The network app uses a shared service so players can organise matches, join rosters, exchange messages and run competitions across devices. This policy covers that network build and the launch website.

Accounts and shared information

The service stores your account identifier, username, display name, selected sports, skill and position preferences, and organiser settings. Passwords and account-recovery codes are stored as hashes, not readable passwords or codes. Signed-in sessions are protected by random tokens; the app keeps its token in the iOS Keychain.

The service also stores matches and venues you create or join, membership and waiting-list records, team assignments, fixtures, results, attendance and payment-status marks, group messages, blocks and support reports. These records support the app's features and account recovery on another device. The app does not process payments or collect card or bank details.

Public match details are discoverable by signed-in players. Private events require an invitation. Group messages are available to authorised event members. Other members can see the profile and roster information needed to organise the event; your password, recovery code and sign-in token are never shared with them.

Location, Maps and Calendar

Your live device location is used on the device to centre maps and calculate distances. It is not uploaded as a location history. The venue address and coordinates you choose for an event are stored with that event; joining or checking in links your account to that venue. Apple Maps processes its map and venue-search requests under Apple's privacy policy.

Calendar access is optional. The app checks clashes on the device and can add a match to your Calendar. Your calendar contents are not uploaded to the Sports Connect service. Calendar events may sync through the accounts you have configured in iOS.

Notifications

Match reminders can be scheduled locally. When remote notifications are configured and you allow them, the service stores an Apple push token to deliver event updates through Apple's notification service. You can turn notifications off in iOS Settings. Shared data also refreshes while the app is open.

Reporting and blocking

Blocks are stored with your account so they apply across devices. Reports send the selected account or content, reason and any explanation to the operator's moderation queue. The operator can review reports, remove content and suspend accounts. Do not include unnecessary private information in report text.

Storage, deletion and sharing

The device caches authorised records for display. The shared service stores active account and event records on its hosting infrastructure. A network connection is required to confirm shared changes; a cached screen does not confirm a new join or message.

Account deletion in Profile removes your account credentials and sessions from the active service, removes your memberships, and removes or anonymises content associated with your account. Shared events may pass to another member so the group can continue. Shared venue details, schedules, other members' messages and aggregate results remain available to the remaining participants; deleted account identifiers are removed from results. Existing device or server backups, if maintained, may retain earlier copies until those backups are replaced or deleted.

Active account and event records are retained while needed to provide the service, until you delete your account or an organiser removes an event. Match chat is limited to its latest 2,000 messages. Production backups are scheduled nightly and rotated after 30 days. A restored backup must replay account-deletion requests before the service is made available.

Signing out clears the account session on this device; it does not delete the shared account. Deleting the app alone does not delete the account. Information you deliberately share through the iOS share sheet is handled by the app or person you choose.

The website and support

The optional website waitlist stores the email address you submit so the operator can send the launch updates you requested. To request removal, contact the address below. Support email is handled by the email providers involved. Please send only the information needed to resolve your request.

Hosting providers process the network information needed to serve requests, such as IP addresses. The app includes no advertising, analytics or third-party tracking SDK and does not sell personal information.

Contact and changes

For support, privacy or data-removal requests, email davispittsrobinson@gmail.com. See the support page for reporting and account deletion. If the app's data practices change, this policy and the App Store privacy answers will be updated.
