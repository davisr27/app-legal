Sports Connect support

Sports Connect is published by Yuta Kodama. For help, email davispittsrobinson@gmail.com. Include your app version and a short description of the problem. Do not send passwords or recovery codes.

How do I report someone?

Long-press the person in a match roster or chat, then choose Report. The report goes to the moderation queue and blocks that person for your account. For urgent content removal, email support with the match name and approximate time.

How do I block someone?

Long-press their roster or chat entry and choose Block. Blocked accounts and their content are hidden from you. You can manage blocked people in Profile.

How do I delete my account?

Open Profile, choose Delete account, and confirm. This removes your account from the active service. Shared events may remain for other participants as described in the privacy policy. If you cannot sign in, email support for help.

Why is my match or message missing?

The app needs a connection to the Sports Connect service. Check your connection and refresh. A private match also requires its invitation. If the problem continues, email support.
