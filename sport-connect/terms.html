Tournament Hosting

Tournament Hosting is an optional monthly auto-renewing subscription that unlocks unlimited tournament creation. Joining tournaments, hosting ordinary matches and creating leagues remain free. Payment is charged to your Apple Account. The subscription automatically renews unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the current period ends. Manage or cancel it in your App Store subscriptions, accessible from Profile → Settings → Tournament Hosting. Restore purchases using the Apple Account and SportNeighbour account you originally subscribed with. There is no free trial or annual commitment.

Access lasts through the paid period, including any billing grace period granted by Apple. When access expires or Apple revokes it, you cannot create new tournaments or convert leagues into tournaments. Existing tournaments remain available to finish. Deleting your SportNeighbour account does not cancel Apple's subscription; cancel separately before deletion. Apple's purchase terms and refund process apply.
