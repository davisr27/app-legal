Payment is charged to your Apple Account. Monthly and yearly subscriptions automatically renew unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the current period ends. Manage or cancel it in your App Store subscriptions, accessible from Profile → Settings → Tournament Hosting. Deleting your Sport Connective account does not cancel Apple's subscription; cancel separately before deletion. Lifetime access is tied to the original Sport Connective account and cannot transfer to a different app account after account deletion. Apple refunds or revocations remove lifetime access.
