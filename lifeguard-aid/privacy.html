Lifeguard Aid Privacy Policy

Last updated 9 October 2026. Lifeguard Aid is published by Yuta Kodama. Davis Robinson is the support contact. This policy describes the current iPhone and iPad beta.

Data handled by the app

The app processes camera frames and videos you select on your device to show person detections and visual risk cues. It does not send footage, detections, session information, or device identifiers to the developer or a server. The app has no account, advertising, analytics, or tracking service. The developer collects no data through the app.

Live mode asks for camera permission. You may deny or revoke it in iOS Settings. The app does not ask for microphone access or record audio. Review opens the movie you choose through the system file picker. A cloud file provider may download that movie under its own privacy practices.

Completed live and video sessions are saved on your device with their start and end times, source name, track and cue counts, and a bounded list of elevated cues. You can review or delete recent sessions and export a CSV report through the iOS file picker. Exporting shares that report with the destination you choose. If you save a pool profile, the app also keeps its zone outlines, camera notes and a small reference frame on the device; you can delete that profile in the zone editor. Raw live video and analysis thumbnails are not saved between launches, and live footage is not recorded or uploaded. The app also stores your acknowledgement of its safety notice on the device. Videos you select remain in their original location. Deleting the app removes its local records.

Camera use

Only use the camera or pool footage where you have permission. Respect the privacy of swimmers, staff and bystanders. On-device processing does not remove your facility's privacy responsibilities.

Support and this website

If you email support, your address and message are received through the email providers and used to respond. Do not email identifiable swimmer footage. You may ask us to delete your correspondence, subject to applicable obligations. The app does not automatically attach footage or diagnostics.

This page is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub processes ordinary web requests needed to deliver it under its privacy statement. Visiting this page is separate from using the on-device app.

Contact and changes

For privacy or support questions, email davispittsrobinson@gmail.com. If the app begins collecting or retaining new data, this policy and the App Store privacy answers will be updated before release.
