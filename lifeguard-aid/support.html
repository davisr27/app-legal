Lifeguard Aid support

Lifeguard Aid is published by Yuta Kodama. Email davispittsrobinson@gmail.com with your device, iOS version, app version and a description of the issue. Do not send identifiable pool footage.

This experimental beta is visual decision support. It is not a certified drowning detector and does not replace trained lifeguards, direct supervision or emergency procedures. Missed detections and false alerts are possible.

How do I start a live session?

Use a physical iPhone or iPad in a fixed position with a clear pool view. Select Live, allow camera access, adjust the pool outline and optional zones, confirm the setup, then start analysis. Recheck the setup whenever the device or camera view moves.

Why is the camera unavailable?

Check camera permission in iOS Settings. Live mode requires a physical device. If another app is using the camera or capture was interrupted, pause and try again.

Why are people missed?

Glare, low light, splashes, crowding, distance, occlusion and submersion can reduce detection. No alert does not mean the water is safe. Keep direct supervision at all times.

How do I review a video?

Select Review and choose a movie through the system file picker. Set and confirm the pool zones on the first frame. The app samples frames; it does not inspect every moment. Keep it open until analysis finishes.

Are sessions saved?

Completed live and video sessions are saved on this device. Open a recent session to review its counts and recorded elevated cues, export a CSV report or delete it. You can also save a pool profile with zone outlines and a small reference frame, then load or delete it in the zone editor. Confirm zones against the current view for every run. Raw live video and analysis thumbnails are not saved between launches.

Support is not an emergency service. Follow your facility's emergency procedures for an emergency.
